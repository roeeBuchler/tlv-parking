# Price-on-sign lots, and Google Maps + easy.co.il harvest (2026-10-04)

## 1. The 29 Ahuzot "בהתאם לשילוט במקום" lots

**Why they say "per sign":** municipal GIS layer 488 classifies 27 of the 29 as
*"חניונים פתוחים לציבור – חניה על פי הוראות התמרור המוצב במקום"*. These are open municipal lots
where the **traffic sign** sets the rules. Layer 970 says "ללא תשלום – יתכנו שינויים" (free, subject to change) for all 27.
The sign can still impose blue-white (street) paid hours or residents-only hours. These lots have no gate tariff the way Ahuzot's paid lots do.

## 2. Broad harvest (scripts in `research/harvest/`)

| Source | How | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Google Maps "חניון" + "parking" | `gmaps_places.mjs`: replays the Maps search call on a ~1 km grid over TLV (651 requests, about 1.5 s apart, no blocks) | **1,802 places; 947 parking places inside TLV**, 325 with a website, 565 rated, 19 tagged "מגרש חניה בחינם" → `gmaps_places.json` |
| Google reviews | browser-pane miner (logged out, so Google shows only about 5 reviews per place), keyword filter | 170 places mined, **77 with price mentions**, condensed to `review_facts.json` (paraphrased, no names) |
| Central Park (44+ lots linked from Google) | `cp_scrape.mjs`: structured JSON embedded in each lot page | **47 lots** with gate *and* app price per day/time window, daily max, live free spaces → `cp_lots.json` |
| Green Parking | `green_scrape.mjs`: WordPress API + lot pages | **25 lots** (some outside TLV) with first hour, per quarter, daily, evening flat → `green_lots.json` |
| easy.co.il | browser only; their API sits behind a Cloudflare bot check, which I didn't try to get around | 100 TLV lots (list cap): names, addresses, tags, owner promos → `easy_list.tsv`. Its "reviews" are imported from Google. |
| GIS layer 488 | one query | 146 resident/open lots with type (contact names and phones stripped) → `gis488.json` |
| Maya Parking | site has no prices (images or "per sign") | skipped |

Promotions spotted: Sarona lot 25% off with Pango from 16:00 (easy ad); Beit Eliyahu 40₪ for 17:00–05:00 (easy owner text).

## 3. What's next (recommended order)

1. **Model the 27 open lots as their own kind, `sign_rules`.** Show "free per municipal data – check the sign" instead of "?".
   Add exceptions from the evidence: 57 (blue-white + residents evening), 68 (part paid), 75 (residents), 138 (conflict).
   Treat them as "free, unverified". One evening of Street View checks covers the 5 uncertain lots.
2. **Import Central Park (47) and Green (25) into `data/lots.json` (M5).** Both are structured enough for the engine.
   Central Park needs the planned `channel` field (gate vs app price). This roughly doubles priced coverage, mostly in the center and Jaffa.
3. **Keep Google data as a research index only.** Google's terms don't allow redistributing Maps content,
   so don't publish Google names, ratings or reviews on the site. Use the 947-place list to *find* lots and operators,
   then source published data from operators, the municipality (layer 556 license registry), or OSM.
4. **Reviews are hints, not prices.** They're anecdotal and often years old. Use them only to flag where to verify.
5. Optional next sources: ipi.co.il (5), parkin.co.il (3), Dizengoff Center page, Sarona (saronaparking.co.il).
6. Signing in to Google would show all reviews and allow sorting by newest. That's your call, and I didn't do it.
